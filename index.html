The Local Grain

A neighbourhood cafe on Erskineville Road serving native-inspired brunch, slow-roasted single-origin coffee and seasonal grain bowls. We cook with what's local, share tables with strangers, and pour a flat white worth crossing the suburb for.

Three things we obsess over

Local & seasonal

Produce sourced from growers within a day's drive, menus rewritten with the seasons.

Roasted on-site

Single-origin beans roasted weekly in-house, pulled within days of roasting.

Native ingredients

Finger lime, saltbush, wattleseed and lemon myrtle woven through the menu.
