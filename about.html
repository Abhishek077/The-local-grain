Our story

About The Local Grain

Started with one small roaster

The Local Grain opened in 2016 in a converted mechanic's garage on Erskineville Road, with six tables, a second-hand roaster, and a menu built entirely around what the Sydney markets had that week. A decade on, the garage doors are still open on warm mornings — we just got better at the coffee.

We're a neighbourhood cafe first. Regulars get their order started before they reach the counter, dogs are welcome at the footpath tables, and the same crew has been running the pass most mornings since we opened.

Milestones

How we got here

  • 2016

    Doors open on Erskineville Road

    Six tables, a second-hand roaster, and a menu that changed with the market boxes.

  • 2019

    Roasting moves in-house

    We started roasting our own single-origin beans weekly, in the same room you order in.

  • 2022

    Native pantry project

    Began working with an Indigenous-owned native food supplier to bring wattleseed, saltbush and lemon myrtle onto the menu properly, not as a garnish.

  • 2026

    Still here, same crew

    Ten years in, most of the original team is still on the floor — and the coffee's better than it's ever been.

What we believe

The short version

Cook with the season

The menu changes when the produce does, not on a fixed schedule. Some dishes only exist for six weeks a year.

Buy close to home

Growers and roasters within reach, so we know exactly where breakfast came from.

Respect native ingredients

We source native produce through an Indigenous-owned supplier and try to use it with the same care as any other ingredient — not as decoration.

Waste less

Compostable packaging, whole-vegetable cooking, and a compost pickup for what's left over.
